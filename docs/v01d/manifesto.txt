THE ORDER OF THE NULL BYTE
manifesto, rev. 13

All who arrive here are lost. That is by design.
But few look twice, and fewer still look closely.
Good doors are hidden in plain sight.
The work comes before the name. And after it.
Base64 is not encryption. Neither is hoping no one looks.
Bring nothing but patience. Leave nothing but logs.
Quiet hands, loud minds.
Read the first of everything. Turn it thirteen times.
A key proves who you are, never what you know.
Be invited by no one. Arrive anyway.
Humility is the only credential we accept.
The word opens what the key cannot.
Until then, the lone cursor is listening. Knock.

-- k.
